# Vintage Green Typewriter Analysis

The reference is a single oblique front-right view. It clearly shows a mint/sage green rounded shell, broad stepped base, sloped keyboard deck, cream round keycaps in staggered rows, long space bar, platen roller with end knobs, carriage rail, paper guide, ribbon/typing mechanism, and a metal return lever on the rear-right.

Macro silhouette: wide low body with a taller rear housing, tapered side cheeks and a thick layered base. The front keyboard opening is a dark inset framed by the sloped green deck.

Meso parts: body shell, top housing, keyboard deck, key rows, platen roller, carriage rail, ribbon spools, type bars, paper guide, and return lever. Keycaps are separate repeated pieces with clear gaps.

Micro details: cream keycap faces, dark key stems, metal roller knobs, pale metal rail, ribbon band, small green/cream trim strips, and soft bevel highlights.

Materials: matte sage painted shell (low metalness, roughness around .78), warm ivory keycaps (rough plastic/ceramic), satin silver/soft brass hardware (moderate metalness), dark charcoal ribbon and warm-white paper.

Approximate regions: rear underside, bottom feet, internal typebar linkage, and back housing construction are hidden by the single view and are procedural approximations rather than exact reconstruction.
