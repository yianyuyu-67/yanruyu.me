# Bankers File Storage Carton - Image Analysis

## Suitability

- Verdict: conditional-pass for a stylized real-time prop. The source is low resolution but the carton silhouette and materials are legible.
- The placement image supplies the scene footprint and confirms that the carton belongs under the desk, centered in the open bay.
- Hidden underside, rear hinge construction and exact corrugation are not visible and are approximated.

## Form and Hierarchy

- Primary form is a rounded rectangular corrugated-cardboard cuboid with an open top.
- Macro parts: box body and hinged lid/flap assembly.
- Meso parts: front rim, side walls, rear hinge, pale file inserts, green stripe band and black label plate.
- Micro features: thin rim seams, repeated file tabs, small recycling mark/side stripe accents.
- The body is upright on the floor, with the front face toward +Z. The lid is attached at the rear (-Z) and rotates upward/backward.

## Materials

- Body: matte warm kraft cardboard, dielectric, roughness about 0.82-0.9, with low-contrast fiber variation.
- Files: pale warm paper, high roughness, slightly lighter value than the cardboard.
- Branding: dark green painted stripes and black label with pale lettering; no metallic response.

## Placement Constraints

- The carton sits on the floor top, centered under the desk opening.
- Its top must remain below the desk underside and its x/z footprint must stay between desk legs and storage bays.
- It is a visual prop only and must not participate in the existing scene raycast.

## Uncertainty

- Exact box depth, lid hinge thickness and corrugation relief are inferred from the single three-quarter image.
- The placement reference labels only the body footprint, so the open lid is kept compact to avoid desk-top intersection.
