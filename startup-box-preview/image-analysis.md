# Image Analysis: startupBox

## Suitability

The reference is a single 1200x1200 image with one clearly isolated wooden shipping crate on a neutral background. It is suitable for a stylized, real-time procedural reconstruction. The front, right side, and top silhouette are readable; the rear, underside, and interior joinery are occluded and therefore approximate.

## Observations

- Macro form: a near-cubic cuboid crate with bilateral vertical side posts, a flat top, and a shallow raised top-rib system.
- Front: a recessed rectangular opening is surrounded by thick vertical and horizontal frame members. A central vertical divider separates two inset boards. The lower edge has a thick sill.
- Top: a pale wood panel is bounded by a perimeter frame. Two raised rectangular battens run front-to-back, with softened edges.
- Right side: a pale side panel carries four red transport pictograms in a two-by-two arrangement.
- Base: four short feet are visible below the lower frame, with the front feet reading as small wooden blocks.
- Surface: light pine-like wood, low-saturation warm cream albedo, matte-to-satin response, subtle linear grain, darker seams at board boundaries.
- Spatial relationships: top battens are attached to the top panel; front panels are embedded behind the front frame; feet are butt-attached below the base frame; side marks are flush decals/plates on the right side.

## Inference and uncertainty

The back panel, underside construction, exact interior depth, and hidden rear feet are not visible. They are modeled as a continuous back board, a flat underside, and four symmetric short feet. These are reasonable structural approximations, not exact measurements.
