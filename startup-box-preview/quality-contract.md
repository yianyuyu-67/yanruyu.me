# Quality Contract

- Definition of done: the preview reads as the supplied pale wooden shipping crate from front, side, top, rear, and three-quarter views, with a cuboid silhouette, recessed front frame, two top battens, four feet, and red side marks.
- Macro requirements: outer shell, front inset assembly, top panel, and foot/base assembly.
- Meso requirements: side posts, top/bottom rails, central divider, two inset panels, top battens, side mark plate.
- Micro requirements: softened board edges, dark seam strips, four red pictogram cells, subtle grain breakup, and independent feet.
- Material requirements: pale wood with low-contrast grain, slightly darker edge/inner wood, red shipping marks, matte roughness 0.78-0.9, metalness 0.
- Review viewpoints: front, left, right, rear, top, and two three-quarter views.
- Blocking failures: missing front recess, missing top battens, cube becoming a solid featureless box, feet floating or penetrating the body, side marks absent, or major components intersecting outside intended embedded contacts.
