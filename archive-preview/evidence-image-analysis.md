# Archive Cabinet Image Analysis

The reference is a four-view measurement sheet: front (upper-left), left side (upper-right), top (lower-left), and rear (lower-right). The hard dimensions are width 9.0, depth 4.0, height 12.3 world units.

Meso structure: upper certificate row (three framed certificates plus dark books), middle row with dark books and two woven archive boxes, lower shelf with mixed books/files and a draped newspaper, and two drawer rows. Shelves are thick horizontal boards joined into the side panels.

Micro details: individual book widths/heights and spine colors, certificate frames, woven box labels, newspaper fold, drawer seams, eight pull handles, small corner fasteners, and softened bevel highlights.

Materials: matte painted yellow-green wood/laminate carcass, darker warm interior cavities, low-metal gray or muted-gold handles, off-white paper, dark blue/green/brown books, tan woven boxes.

Uncertainty: the rear view gives a complete back panel but hides joinery; drawer runners and exact shelf fasteners are approximated procedurally. The sheet is a composite, so separated views are not treated as one continuous silhouette for pixel scoring.
