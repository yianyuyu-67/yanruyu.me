# Quality Contract

- Definition of done: the browser render reads as the referenced vintage green/gold articulated lamp from front, side, rear, top, and three-quarter views; dimensions remain within +/-0.5 cm of the supplied sheet; every required component is independently addressable and animation-ready.
- Macro minimum: 5 systems (base, stem, shade, arm, cord/switch).
- Meso minimum: 10 systems (base steps/collars, stem collars/finial, dome/interior/rim, arm links/joints, switch/cord).
- Micro minimum: 12 grouped details (rings, finial, joints, rim, inner reflector, switch pull, cord bend, contact shadows, etc.).
- Repetition: concentric stepped radial base and collar/rim ring systems.
- Materials: independent green metal, brass, ivory reflector, rubber cord; each has albedo, roughness, metalness and localized highlight intent.
- Review viewpoints: front, left, right, rear, top, three-quarter; diagnostic checks include ground contact, attachments and self-intersection.
- Blocking failures: wrong base/shade diameter, missing arm span, flat or absent dome, detached joints, floating cord, bottom below y=0, or unlabelled inferred hidden structure.
